---
title: "Old share links without an expiry date have been disabled"
date: "2026-10-08"
summary: "To keep shared plans safer, we disabled old share links that had no expiry date. Reissue the link if you still need it."
---

## What changed

- Old share links (trips and date polls) that have no expiry date can no longer be opened
- A share link is valid for 7 days after it is issued. Once it expires, it can no longer be opened either
- As before, only the owner of a trip (or date poll) can issue or reissue a share link. Editors and viewers do not see the share link

## If you still need an old link

The owner can reissue the link from "Share link" on the trip (or date poll) to get a new link with an expiry date. The previous link stays closed even after reissuing, so please send the new link to the people you want to share with.

As always, please send your feedback from the feedback form in the settings screen.
