## New features

### Calendar (.ics) export

We added "iCal (.ics)" as a format option on the export screen. Import the exported file into Google Calendar or Apple Calendar and your trip schedules appear as calendar events.

It exports each day's main-plan schedules. Schedules with a time become timed events, and schedules without a time become all-day events. Overnight schedules such as a hotel checkout carry the correct end date.

The existing Excel (.xlsx) and CSV exports are still available, so pick whichever fits your needs.
