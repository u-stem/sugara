## New features

### Record a transit fare on transport schedules

On a schedule with the Transport category, you can now enter a transit fare alongside the departure, arrival, and transport method. The amount is in the trip's currency and appears on the transport card in the timeline.

The transit fare is a lightweight field for planning estimates. It is not linked to the settlement on the Expenses tab, so if you want it included in the actual split, add it as an expense on the Expenses tab as before.

### Bicycle added as a transport method

You can now choose "Bicycle" as a transport method. The options are train, shinkansen, bus, taxi, walk, car, bicycle, and airplane.
