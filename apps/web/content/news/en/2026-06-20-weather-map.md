## Improvements

### See where a region is on the map

We added a map of Japan to the top of each region's detail screen in the Weather tool.

- The region you are viewing is highlighted on the map, so you can tell at a glance where it is in Japan
- Areas that are subdivided, such as Hokkaido and Okinawa, are shown so you can tell their individual sections apart

### About the data

- The map data is processed from the "JMA Disaster Prevention Information Area Dataset" (created by NII) / JMA GIS Data, licensed under CC BY 4.0
