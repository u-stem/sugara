Here's a summary of our recent updates.

## New features

### Articles

You can now write travel notes and recommended spots in Markdown with the new Articles feature. Headings, lists, links, and quotes are supported (images are not).

- Organize with **tags** and add **likes**
- Choose visibility: 'Private,' 'Friends Only,' or 'Public'
- Link an article to a trip, and its members can see it — even a 'Private' one — within that trip. Read linked articles in place from the trip's 'Articles' tab (or 'Related articles' in the three-dot menu on mobile)
- Access your articles from 'Articles' in the header (or the bottom menu on mobile)

### Calendar (.ics) export

We added "iCal (.ics)" as a format on the export screen. Import the file into Google Calendar or Apple Calendar and each day's main-plan schedules appear as calendar events. The existing Excel (.xlsx) and CSV exports are still available.

### Ferry, ropeway, and cable car transport methods

You can now choose "Ferry," "Ropeway," or "Cable Car" as a transport method, so trips to islands, mountains, and sightseeing spots can be recorded more accurately.

## Improvements

### Links in day memos

URLs written in a day's memo now turn into blue links you can tap (or click) to open. Editing a memo has also moved to a **pencil icon** next to the memo, so opening a link no longer starts editing.

Please keep sending us your feedback from the Settings page.
