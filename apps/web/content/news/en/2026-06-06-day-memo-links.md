## Improvements

### Links in day memos

`http` / `https` URLs written in a day's memo now turn into blue links you can tap (or click) to open directly. Links open in a new tab.

Editing a memo has moved to a **pencil icon** next to the memo. Previously tapping anywhere on the memo opened the editor; now tapping a link and editing the memo are separate, so opening a link no longer starts editing.

Please keep sending us your feedback from the Settings page.
