## New features

### Articles

You can now write travel notes and recommended spots in Markdown with the new Articles feature. Headings, lists, links, quotes, and code are supported (images are not).

- Organize with **tags** and add **likes**
- Choose visibility: 'Private,' 'Friends Only,' or 'Public'
- Access your articles from 'Articles' in the header (or My Page on mobile)

### Linking to trips and sharing

Articles can be linked to a trip. Members of a linked trip can see the article — even a 'Private' one — within that trip. Read linked articles in place from the trip's 'Articles' tab (or 'Related articles' in the three-dot menu on mobile).

A 'Private' article not linked to any trip stays a personal note just for you.

Please keep sending us your feedback from the Settings page.
