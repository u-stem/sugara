## Enhancements

### Switch the settlement display mode

The settlement list on the Expenses tab can now be switched between two display modes. Either way, every member's final net balance is identical — only the payment routes shown differ.

- **By payer**: Shows amounts repaid directly to whoever paid for each expense, with debts between the same pair offset against each other. You can follow "who covered whose share" at a glance.
- **Minimal**: Shows amounts calculated to minimize the number of transfers, so you make the fewest payments.

Previously only "Minimal" was available. In minimal mode, if the person who covered for you ends up on the receiving side overall, no "you → them" transfer appears — which can be confusing when you only look at the result. Choosing "By payer" lets you see those intermediate debts too.

Your chosen display mode is remembered on your device. Settlement checks (completion records) can still be made in "Minimal" mode.
