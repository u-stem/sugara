## New features

### Check the weekly forecast for regions across Japan

We added "Weather" to the "Tools" section of your profile page.

- Browse the forecast for regions across Japan, grouped by area
- Select a region to see up to 7 days of weather icons, high/low temperatures, and chance of rain
- It is a standalone tool you can use anytime, not tied to any trip

### About the data

- Based on the weekly forecast published by the Japan Meteorological Agency (source: JMA)
- Fetched and stored automatically once a day, so it loads instantly. The latest issue time is shown at the bottom of each region's detail screen

As always, please send your feedback from the feedback form in the settings screen.
