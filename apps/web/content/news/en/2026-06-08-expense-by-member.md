## New feature

### Per-member breakdown

Open "Show Details" under the total spending on the Expenses tab and you'll now see a "By Member" breakdown in addition to the existing "By Category" one. It shows the total amount allocated to each member (their share of the spending) in the trip currency.

- Expenses entered in a foreign currency are converted to the trip currency, so the per-member totals add up to the total spending
- It is hidden on solo trips with a single member, since it would just repeat the total

## Enhancement

### Details now available on solo trips

Previously "Show Details" only appeared once a settlement (a transfer) was needed, so recording expenses on a solo trip gave you no category breakdown. Now, as long as an expense has a category set, you can open its breakdown on a solo trip too.
